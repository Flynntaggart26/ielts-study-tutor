# Websites & Apps — How to Use Each

## Strategy & Lessons
- **IELTS Liz (ieltsliz.com)** — Use for: Task 1 letter/report structures, Speaking Part 2 cue-card ideas. Do: 1 lesson/day, copy model sentences into Anki.
- **IELTS Simon** — Use for: daily 5-min Task 2 idea generation. Ex-examiner style, concise.
- **IELTS Advantage Blog** — Use for: free video course + paid correction. Best Task 2 band 9 breakdowns.

## Interactive Practice
- **Road to IELTS** — 300+ activities + 9 mocks. Access free via many libraries/British Council registration. Best CBT UI.
- **Write & Improve (Cambridge)** — Paste Task 1/2 → instant CEFR level + corrections. Use before paying for human correction.
- **IELTS Online Tests** — Volume drills. Warning: some Reading keys disputed — cross-check with Cambridge.

## Vocab & Grammar Tools
- **Anki + AWL (Academic Word List) deck** — 10 new cards/day, review daily. Only add collocations, not single words.
- **Quizlet IELTS sets** — Good for topic sets (environment, education).
- **Grammarly / Ludwig.guru** — Self-edit Writing drafts for articles/prepositions.

## Apps
- **IELTS Prep App (British Council)** — daily tips + practice tests, offline.
- **E2Language / Magoosh Apps** — structured paths, push reminders.
- **ELSA Speak** — pronunciation score per sound. 10 min/day fixes Part 1 clarity.

Rule: max 3 apps installed. Delete if unused after 7 days.
