# Books Roadmap by Level

## B1 (Band 4.5-5.5) → Foundation
1. `The Official Cambridge Guide to IELTS` — start to finish
2. `Get Ready for IELTS (Collins: Reading, Writing, Listening, Speaking)` — 4 thin books
3. `English Grammar in Use (Murphy, Intermediate)` — units 1-60

Goal: understand format + build 3000-word base.

## B2 (Band 5.5-6.5) → Technique
1. `Cambridge IELTS 10-13` (1 test/week untimed → timed)
2. `Reading for IELTS / Listening for IELTS (Collins)`
3. `Cambridge Vocabulary for IELTS`
4. `Grammar for IELTS (Cambridge)` — first half

Goal: 3 skills at 6.0, Writing at 6.0 with clear structure.

## C1 (Band 7.0-8.0) → Precision
1. `Cambridge IELTS 14-18`
2. `IELTS Advantage Writing Skills`
3. `English Collocations in Use Advanced` + `Academic Vocabulary in Use`
4. `Advanced Grammar in Use (Murphy)`

Goal: less common vocab, complex sentences, error-free Task 1.

## C2 (Band 8.0-9.0) → Polish
1. `Cambridge IELTS 18-19` + Official Practice Materials Vol 2
2. Sam McCarter Academic Writing + examiner reports
3. Native materials: The Economist, Nature, BBC Radio 4

Tip: libraries often have full Cambridge sets + Road to IELTS free. Don't buy all — buy 1 Writing + 1 Vocab book, borrow mocks.
