# YouTube Channels & Podcasts

## YouTube (Subscribe to max 3)

1. **E2 IELTS** — https://www.youtube.com/@E2IELTS
   - Best for: live classes, Listening/Reading walkthroughs, Speaking mocks.
   - Playlist to start: "IELTS Beginner Course" → "Band 7+ Writing".

2. **IELTS Advantage (Chris Pell)** — https://www.youtube.com/@IELTSAdvantage
   - Best for: Writing Task 2 structures that actually get 7+.
   - Start: "How to write a Band 7 essay in 40 minutes".

3. **AcademicEnglishHelp (IELTS VIP)** — https://www.youtube.com/@academicenglishhelp
   - Best for: Speaking Band 9 samples with examiner comments.

4. **IELTS Liz** — short 5-10 min tips, Task 1 Academic.
5. **IELTS Simon / Fastrack IELTS** — quick Reading tactics.

How to watch: 1 video → pause → attempt task yourself → compare. Never binge-watch.

## Podcasts & Listening Diet (15-30 min/day)

- **BBC 6 Minute English** — perfect length, transcript included. Shadow 1 episode/day.
- **The English We Speak (BBC)** — idioms for Speaking Part 2/3.
- **TED Talks Daily (6-12 min)** — Part 4 lecture simulation. Listen once, note-take, summarize in 3 sentences.
- **The Economist / BBC Global News** — C1 speed + academic vocab.
- **Luke's English Podcast** — British humour + natural speech, B2-C1.

Tip: use 1.0x speed only. IELTS is 1.0x with accents (UK/AU/NZ/CA).
