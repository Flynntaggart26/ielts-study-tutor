# Best Sources for IELTS — Master Curated List

> 40+ sources rated by usefulness, cost, and level. Updated for 2026. Academic + General labels included.

**Legend:** ✅ Free | 💰 Paid | 📚 Book | 🌐 Site/App | 🎥 Video | 🎧 Audio | 🏛️ Official
**Level:** B1 (Band 4.5-5.5) → B2 (5.5-6.5) → C1 (7.0-8.0) → C2 (8.0-9.0)

## 📚 Tier 2 — Best Books by Skill

**Writing:**
- `Task 2: IELTS Advantage Writing Skills` (C1) — best Task 2 structure.
- `Grammar for IELTS (Cambridge)` — fixes 80% of Task 2 grammar errors.
- `Academic Writing Practice for IELTS (Sam McCarter)` — Task 1 Academic mastery.

**Reading / Listening:**
- `Reading for IELTS (Collins, Anneli Williams)` — B1-B2 technique drills.
- `Listening for IELTS (Collins)` — section-by-section tactics + audio.

**Vocab:**
- `Cambridge Vocabulary for IELTS + Advanced` — topic-based, with tests.
- `English Collocations in Use (Intermediate + Advanced)` — essential for 7+.
- `Check Your English Vocabulary for IELTS (Wyatt)` — quick revision.

See full level roadmap: [`books-by-level.md`](books-by-level.md).

## 🎥 Tier 4 — YouTube & Podcasts

Top channels (no fluff, examiner-led):
1. **E2 IELTS** — full test walkthroughs, live classes.
2. **IELTS Advantage (Chris Pell)** — Writing 7+ structures.
3. **AcademicEnglishHelp / IELTS VIP** — Speaking band 9 samples.
4. **IELTS Liz** — short strategy clips.
5. **BBC 6 Minute English / The English We Speak** — B2-C1 listening + vocab (10 min/day).

Podcasts for Listening Part 4 + vocab: `TED Talks Daily`, `The Economist Podcasts`, `Luke's English Podcast` (British).

Full list: [`youtube-podcasts.md`](youtube-podcasts.md).

## 📝 Tier 5 — Practice Tests (Where to Take Them)

Ranked by authenticity:
1. Cambridge 15-19 (paper) — gold standard
2. British Council / IDP computer-delivered mocks (free at test centres)
3. Road to IELTS mock interface — best CBT simulation
4. IELTS Online Tests — good for volume, double-check reading keys

Avoid: random PDFs with no answer explanations, pre-2015 tests for Writing (criteria shifted).

Full guide: [`practice-tests.md`](practice-tests.md).

## 🚫 Avoid List

- Any "guaranteed Band 8 in 3 days" course.
- Memorized Task 2 essays / Speaking scripts (examiners penalize).
- Outdated blogs (pre-2020) for Writing Task 1 format.
- Apps with no British/Australian accents for Listening.

## How to Use This List

- Week 1-2: Only Tier 1 + IELTS Liz + 1 YouTube channel.
- Week 3+: Add vocab book + Write & Improve + 1 mock/week.
- Last 2 weeks: Cambridge mocks only, timed, no new sources.
