# Self-Assessment & Diagnostic

Do this Day 0 (2 hours). Be honest — wrong level = wrong plan.

## Step 1 — Timed Mini-Mock (90 min)
- Listening: Cambridge test, Part 1-2 only (20 min). Score /20 → x2 estimate.
- Reading: 1 Academic passage (20 min). Score /13 → scale.
- Writing: Task 2, 40 min, 250+ words (any past question).
- Speaking: record Part 2 cue card (2 min) + 4 Part 1 Qs.

## Step 2 — Score

|-------|-----|------------|
| Listening | raw/20 | ___ → est. Band ___ |
| Reading | raw/13 | ___ → est. Band ___ |

| Speaking | `templates/speaking-self-evaluation.md` | F__/L__/G__/P__ → ___ |

## Step 3 — Decide

- All ≥ target - 0.5 → take `crash-7-days.md` + book test.
- Gap 0.5-1.0 → `4-week-band-6-to-7.md` or `8-week-zero-to-hero.md`.
- Gap >1.0 or B1 → `12-week-comprehensive.md`, don't book yet.
- Writing/Speaking lowest → 50% time there (see `tutor/how-to-study-guide.md`).

Save scores in `templates/study-tracker.csv` Day 0 row. Re-test every 2 weeks, same conditions.
