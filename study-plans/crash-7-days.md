# 7-Day Crash Plan (Band 6.5+ → hold score, fix exam technique)

> 3h/day. Not for learning English — for maximizing current level.

- **Day 1:** Diagnostic (see self-assessment) + read `tutor/common-mistakes.md`. 1 Listening + 1 Reading timed.
- **Day 2:** Writing Task 2 template drill (2 essays, same structure) + correction.
- **Day 3:** Speaking Parts 1-3 marathon (6 cue cards, record all) + Task 1 overview drill (4 overviews, no full reports).
- **Day 4:** Full mock (L+R+W timed) + error log.
- **Day 5:** Fix weakest (usually Task 2 or Part 3) — rewrite + re-record.
- **Day 6:** Second full mock (CBT interface if computer test) + light vocab review.
- **Day 7:** No new material. Review error log, 1 passage + 1 essay outline, sleep early. Documents ready.

Daily: 10 min Anki (only your errors), 15 min BBC 6-min shadowing.
