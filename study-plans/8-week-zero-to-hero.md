# 8-Week Plan: B2 → 7.0-7.5 Zero to Hero (1.5-2h/day)

## Weeks 1-2 — Build Base
- Official Guide units + Collins Reading/Listening books.
- Grammar: articles, tenses. Vocab: education + environment sets.
- 1 passage + 1 listening part/day untimed → timed end Week 2.

## Weeks 3-4 — Skill Up
- Task 2 every Tue/Fri, Task 1 every Mon. Speaking 3x/week recorded.
- Reading: 3 passages/week timed. Listening: full tests 1x/week.
- Mock 1 end Week 4.

## Weeks 5-6 — Intensify
- 2 essays/week + rewrite, 4 speaking recordings/week.
- Full Listening + Reading weekly timed.
- Vocab: technology, health, work sets. Grammar: passives, conditionals.

## Weeks 7-8 — Mock Season
- Mock/week full timed (Cambridge 17-18), review next day.
- Fix error-log top 5. No new books.
- Final week light, exam simulation (morning, timed, no phone).

Milestones: W2: R 6.0 timed, W4: W 6.5 corrected, W6: 3 skills at 7.0, W8: 3 mocks at target.
