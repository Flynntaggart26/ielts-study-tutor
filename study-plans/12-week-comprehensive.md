# 12-Week Comprehensive: B1 → 7.5 (1-2h/day, working adults)

## Phase 1 (W1-4): Foundations
- Get Ready for IELTS (Collins) + Official Guide Part 1.
- Grammar in Use 3x/week. Anki 10/day. BBC 6-min daily.
- No full mocks. Short drills only. Goal: understand format, 3000-word base.

## Phase 2 (W5-8): Technique
- Cambridge 13-15, 1 test/fortnight. Task 2 1x/week + Task 1 1x/2 weeks.
- Speaking Part 1-2 fluency. Reading T/F/NG + headings mastery.
- Mock 1 (W8). Goal: 3 skills 6.0.

## Phase 3 (W9-12): Performance
- Cambridge 16-19, 1 mock/week. 2 essays/week + correction.
- Speaking Part 3 + complex grammar. Vocab: collocations only.
- W11-12: exam simulation, error-log review, taper.

If full-time (4h/day), compress to 8 weeks. If <1h/day, extend Phase 1 to 6 weeks — don't rush to mocks.
