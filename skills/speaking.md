# Speaking — Parts 1-3 Guide (11-14 min)

## Format
Part 1 (4-5 min): familiar topics, 2-3 topics, short answers (2-4 sentences).
Part 2 (3-4 min): cue card, 1 min prep, 2 min talk.
Part 3 (4-5 min): abstract discussion, longer answers.

## Scoring (each 25%)
Fluency/Coherence, Lexical Resource, Grammar, Pronunciation. Band 7 needs all four at 7 — one weak area caps you.

## Method
- **Part 1:** Answer + reason + example. No one-word answers, no 1-min monologues.
- **Part 2:** Use 1-min prep for keywords only (who/what/why + 2 story details). Talk in past → present → future. Fill 2 min even if nervous — keep talking.
- **Part 3:** Opinion → explain → example → alternative view. Use hedging (tend to, likely, in many cases).

## Daily Drill (15 min)
- Day A: Record 1 Part 1 set (phone), listen, fix hesitations/fillers.
- Day B: 1 cue card timed, transcribe 1 min, correct grammar.
- Day C: Shadow BBC 6-min (pronunciation/intonation).

## Band 7 Phrases (natural, not memorized)
- Opinion: "I'm inclined to think...", "On balance..."
- Comparing: "Whereas in my country...", "Compared to a decade ago..."
- Hedging: "It depends on...", "Not necessarily, but..."

## Common Mistakes
- Memorized answers (examiner changes question slightly → you fail).
- Silent pauses >3 sec. Use fillers: "That's an interesting question...", "Let me think...".
- Simple sentences only in Part 3. Force 1 conditional + 1 relative clause per answer.
