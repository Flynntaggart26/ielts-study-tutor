# Reading — Strategy Guide (Academic + General)

## Format (60 min, 40 Q, no extra transfer)
Academic: 3 long passages (2200-2750 words). General: shorter, more texts. Types: T/F/NG, matching headings, MCQ, gap-fill, sentence completion.

## Core Principle
IELTS Reading is **search + paraphrase**, not full comprehension. Don't read everything first.

## Tactics by Type
- **T/F/NG:** Find sentence, compare meaning. FALSE = contradicts. NG = not stated (don't infer).
- **Matching Headings:** Read headings first, skim paragraph first/last sentence, match main idea not detail.
- **Gap-fill / Sentence completion:** Predict word type, scan for paraphrase, respect word limit.
- **MCQ:** Eliminate 2 obviously wrong, verify remaining 2 against text line.

## Time System (Academic)
Passage 1: 17 min → Passage 2: 20 min → Passage 3: 23 min. If stuck >2 min on 1 Q, guess + mark + move.

## Drill Plan (3x/week)
- Skim/scan: 1 passage untimed with highlighter for paraphrases (20 min) + timed redo (17 min).
- Vocab: extract 10 academic collocations per passage into Anki (e.g. "pose a threat", "conduct research").
- Review: re-do all wrong Qs after 3 days without looking at answers.

## General Training Difference
Section 1-2 are ads/notices — speed matters. Practice skimming for numbers/dates. Section 3 = Academic-like.
