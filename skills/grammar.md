# Grammar Quick Fix Guide

Target the 7 structures that cause 90% of Band 6 caps. Master in this order:

1. **Articles** — `the environment` (general concept with the), `a solution` (first mention) → `the solution` (referred). No article for plural generals: `Governments should...` not `The governments`.
2. **Plurals** — Listening + Writing: `advantage vs advantages`, `phenomenon → phenomena`.
3. **Tense** — Task 1 past years = past simple. Task 2 general truths = present simple. Don't mix without reason.
4. **Complex sentences** — Combine: `Although technology connects people, it can reduce face-to-face interaction.` Aim 60% complex in Task 2.
5. **Passive** — Processes + formal tone: `Electricity is generated by...`
6. **Prepositions** — `increase in, responsible for, depend on, account for, in contrast to`.
7. **Word formation** — `environment (n) → environmental (adj) → environmentally (adv)`. Check suffix every time.

Daily: write 5 sentences using today's structure, get corrected, add best 2 to Anki.
