# Listening — Strategy Guide

## Format (30 min + transfer)
Part 1: conversation (booking) → Part 2: monologue (map/plan) → Part 3: discussion (academic) → Part 4: lecture. 40 Q, each 1 mark. 1 play only.

## Band Killers
- Plural vs singular, postcode/numbers, distractors ("No, it's Thursday, not Tuesday").
- Map labelling + multiple choice (Part 2/3) — most miss here.

## Method
1. **Predict:** Use 30-sec prep to underline keywords, guess answer type (number/date/name).
2. **Listen for paraphrase:** You hear "cost", question says "price/fee". Train synonyms.
3. **Write + keep listening:** Don't fix spelling while audio runs. Mark and move.
4. **Transfer check:** plurals, a/an, capitalisation, word limit (ONE WORD ONLY = hyphenated counts as one).

## Drill Plan (3x/week, 30 min)
- Mon: Part 1+2 timed → transcript review, list 10 paraphrases.
- Wed: Part 3 multiple-choice — pause, justify why wrong options are wrong.
- Fri: Part 4 note-taking — listen once, summarize in 3 sentences aloud.

## Accent Training
BBC 6 Min + E2 listening tests (AU/NZ accents). Shadow 5 min/day.

## Error Log Template
`Test / Q# / My answer / Correct / Why (missed keyword? spelling? distractor?)`
Fix pattern after 5 tests, not after 1.
