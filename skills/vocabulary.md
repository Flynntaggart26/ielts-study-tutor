# Vocabulary & Grammar — High-ROI Lists

## Vocabulary Principle
Examiners score **collocations + precision**, not rare words. 10 natural collocations > 30 memorized "big words".

## Must-Know Topic Sets (with 3 collocations each)
- **Education:** pursue higher education, core curriculum, academic performance
- **Environment:** carbon emissions, renewable energy, pose a threat to biodiversity
- **Technology:** bridge the digital divide, widespread adoption, privacy concerns
- **Health:** sedentary lifestyle, balanced diet, mental well-being
- **Work:** job satisfaction, career prospects, work-life balance
- **Crime:** deter crime, reoffend, rehabilitate offenders
- **Globalization:** cultural homogeneity, economic disparity, cross-border trade

Full list + example sentences: build from your own essays + Cambridge Vocabulary for IELTS.

## Anki Rules
- Front: cue (e.g. "environment + threat") → Back: collocation + example sentence you wrote.
- 10 new/day max, review daily. Delete leeches after 5 fails, relearn via sentence.

## Grammar for Band 7 (in order of ROI)
1. Articles (a/the/Ø) — #1 error source.
2. Subject-verb agreement + plurals.
3. Tenses (past vs present perfect for Task 1 trends).
4. Relative clauses (which/who/where) — 2 per essay.
5. Conditionals (If governments invested..., ... would...).
6. Passive (was built, has been linked to).
7. Hedging (may, tends to, is likely to) — essential for Task 2/3.

Drill: `Grammar for IELTS` 1 unit/day, apply immediately in that day's writing/speaking.
