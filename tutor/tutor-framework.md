# Tutor Framework (for Human Tutors & Study Partners)

## Diagnostic (First Lesson, 60 min)

1. 15 min: interview (goal, date, past scores, hours/day).
2. 20 min: mini-mock (1 Reading passage + Task 2 outline + 3 Part 1 Qs recorded).
3. 15 min: error triage — top 3 blockers (e.g. articles, T/F/NG, fluency pauses).
4. 10 min: assign plan + homework policy.

Output: 1-page plan with target date + tracker link.

## Lesson Templates

**60-min Writing lesson:**
- 10 review homework corrections (student explains fixes)
- 20 timed writing (Task 1 or 2)
- 25 live correction (think-aloud, max 5 patterns, not every error)
- 5 assign rewrite + 10 collocations

**60-min Speaking lesson:**
- 10 Part 1 warm-up with instant pronunciation fix
- 20 Part 2 (1-min prep, 2-min talk, transcribe 1 min together)
- 25 Part 3 discussion (push for conditionals/hedging)
- 5 record homework task

**90-min Mock review:**
- Student pre-scores with checklists, tutor validates, focus on strategy not just answers.

## Feedback Rules

- Max 5 patterns per session. More overwhelms.
- Always: correct form → why → student self-corrects → new sentence.
- End with 1 win + 2 next actions.
- Use `feedback-templates.md` sheets so student can revise alone.

## Homework Policy

- Writing students: 2 tasks/week + rewrite (no rewrite = no new task).
- Speaking students: 3 recordings/week (min 2 min each).
- All: Anki daily screenshot 1x/week.

## Progress Tracking

Every 4 lessons: 1 timed section re-test, update tracker, adjust plan. If no +0.5 in 6 weeks at 8h/week, change focus (usually needs more feedback, not more hours).
